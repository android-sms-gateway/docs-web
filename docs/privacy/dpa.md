# ⚖️ Data Processing Agreement

This Data Processing Agreement (**"DPA"**) sets out the terms under which personal data is processed on behalf of customers of the SMSGate services in accordance with **Article 28 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR)**. It is concluded between the **Controller** (the customer who determines the purposes of processing) and the **Processor** (the individual who operates the SMSGate services), and forms part of the contract for the use of the services.

Items shown in `[brackets]` are completed when the agreement is concluded. To execute this DPA, contact [privacy@sms-gate.app](mailto:privacy@sms-gate.app).

See also: [Privacy Policy](./policy.md) · [End-to-End Encryption](./encryption.md)

!!! warning "Standard contractual clauses"
    The international data transfer terms in **Annex IV** incorporate the Standard Contractual Clauses of **Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 of 4 June 2021** by reference (Module Two: controller to processor). When signing, both parties should keep a copy of that Decision alongside this DPA.

## 🎯 1. Subject matter and scope

This DPA governs the processing of personal data by the Processor on behalf of the Controller in connection with the Controller's use of the SMSGate services (Cloud Server mode or Private Server mode with cloud push relay), as described in **Annex I**. Capitalised terms not defined here have the meanings given in the GDPR.

## 📌 2. Documented instructions

The Processor shall process personal data only on **documented instructions** from the Controller (Art. 28(3)(a) GDPR), unless required to do otherwise by Union or Member State law, in which case the Processor shall inform the Controller of that legal requirement before processing, unless that law prohibits such information on important grounds of public interest.

Documented instructions comprise:

1. this DPA and Annexes I–IV;
2. the Controller's configuration of the services (including retention windows and encryption settings);
3. further written instructions sent to [privacy@sms-gate.app](mailto:privacy@sms-gate.app) and confirmed after the requester has proven control of the Controller's account.

The Processor shall immediately inform the Controller if, in the Processor's opinion, an instruction infringes the GDPR or other Union or Member State data protection law.

## 🔐 3. Confidentiality

The Processor shall ensure that persons authorised to process the personal data have committed themselves to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality, and that access is limited to what is necessary for the performance of the services (Art. 28(3)(b) GDPR). The service is operated by a natural person; any assistant engaged for maintenance or support is bound by equivalent obligations before access is granted.

## 🛡️ 4. Security of processing

The Processor shall implement the technical and organisational measures set out in **Annex II**, having regard to the state of the art, the costs of implementation and the nature, scope, context and purposes of processing, as well as the risk to the rights and freedoms of natural persons (Art. 28(3)(c) and Art. 32 GDPR).

## 🧩 5. Sub-processors

The Controller grants a **general written authorisation** for the engagement of the sub-processors listed in **Annex III** (Art. 28(2) GDPR).

1. The Processor shall inform the Controller of any intended addition or replacement of a sub-processor at least **[30] days** before the change takes effect, by publishing the updated Annex III and notifying the contact address given in Annex I.
2. The Controller may object to a proposed change in writing within that period. If the objection cannot be resolved, the Controller may terminate the affected services without penalty for the portion of the service that relies on that sub-processor.
3. The Processor shall impose on each sub-processor data protection obligations that are no less protective than those set out in this DPA, and remains fully liable to the Controller for the performance of the sub-processor's obligations (Art. 28(4) GDPR).

## 🤝 6. Assistance with data subject rights

Taking into account the nature of the processing, the Processor shall assist the Controller by appropriate technical and organisational measures, insofar as this is possible, with the fulfilment of the Controller's obligation to respond to requests exercising data subject rights (Art. 28(3)(e) GDPR).

Where end-to-end encryption is enabled and the client has verified the device public key (see [Encryption](./encryption.md)), the Processor cannot access message content or recipient phone numbers; the Processor shall provide the metadata that is available to it (message state history, timestamps, device association) and shall direct data subjects to the Controller, who determines the content of their data.

## 🚨 7. Personal data breaches

The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting the Controller's personal data, at the contact address given in Annex I, and shall provide the information necessary for the Controller to meet its obligations under Articles 33 and 34 GDPR (Art. 28(3)(f) GDPR). The Processor shall assist the Controller with any resulting communication to supervisory authorities and data subjects, taking into account the nature of the processing.

## 🗑️ 8. Deletion or return of personal data

At the end of the provision of the services, the Processor shall, at the Controller's choice, **delete or return** all personal data and delete existing copies, unless Union or Member State law requires storage (Art. 28(3)(g) GDPR).

1. Deletion is the default: live personal data is deleted within **[30] days** after termination of the services.
2. If the Controller chooses return, data is provided in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format where technically feasible, before deletion.
3. Backup copies are erased automatically within **12 days** of the deletion of the corresponding live data, so all existing copies are removed within **[42] days** of termination (see Annex I).
4. If the Processor is legally required to retain specific data, the Processor shall inform the Controller of that requirement unless the law prohibits such information, and shall process the retained data only for that legal purpose.

## 🔍 9. Audit and information

The Processor shall make available to the Controller all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with the obligations laid down in Art. 28 GDPR and shall allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections, conducted by the Controller or another auditor mandated by the Controller (Art. 28(3)(h) GDPR).

1. The Controller may request compliance documentation (this DPA, Annexes I–III, and confirmation of the measures in Annex II) at any time.
2. Audits take place no more than **once per year**, with at least **[30] days** prior written notice, during normal working hours, and the auditor is bound by confidentiality.
3. Taking into account that the service is operated by an individual, the Processor may satisfy an audit request by documentary evidence or questionnaire before any inspection; an inspection occurs only if documentary evidence is insufficient or a personal data breach has taken place.

## 🌍 10. International transfers

Where the processing involves transfers of personal data to a third country, the transfers are governed by the **Standard Contractual Clauses incorporated in Annex IV** (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914, Module Two: controller to processor), which form an integral part of this DPA.

If the Processor determines that it can no longer comply with the Standard Contractual Clauses due to a change in the law or practice of the third country of import, the Processor shall promptly inform the Controller, and the Controller may suspend the transfer or terminate the services.

## ⚖️ 11. General provisions

1. **Term.** This DPA enters into force on the effective date and co-terminates with the agreement for the use of the services.
2. **Amendments.** Changes are agreed in writing; exchange of signed copies or of confirmations by email is sufficient.
3. **Relationship.** The Processor acts as an independent processor and not as an agent, employee or representative of the Controller.
4. **Precedence.** Mandatory requirements of Art. 28(3) GDPR apply regardless of this DPA. In the event of a conflict between this DPA and the Standard Contractual Clauses in Annex IV, the Standard Contractual Clauses prevail.
5. **Governing law and jurisdiction.** This DPA (other than its Annex IV) is governed by the law of **[Member State]**, and the courts of **[Member State]** have exclusive jurisdiction, without prejudice to the rules on jurisdiction applicable to data subjects under the Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 🛡️ Annex II — Technical and organisational measures

| #   | Measure                                 | Implementation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Encryption in transit                   | TLS termination at the edge (Cloudflare) with Full (strict) encryption between edge and origin, and TLS at the reverse proxy (Traefik) for all public endpoints                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 2   | End-to-end encryption (optional)        | Device-paired RSA-2048 (OAEP) + AES-256-GCM; private key stays in the Android Keystore; server stores only the public key (see [Encryption](./encryption.md))                                                                                                                                                                               |
| 3   | Pseudonymisation of plaintext (SHA-256) | Message content and recipient phone numbers of non-E2E messages are pseudonymised by SHA-256 hashing about one minute after processing                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 4   | Password security                       | Passwords stored as bcrypt hashes; short-lived JWT access tokens (15 minutes) with refresh-token rotation (30 days); expired tokens purged                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 5   | Access control                          | Infrastructure access is limited to the Processor; access credentials are held in secure configuration and never in source code                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 6   | Physical security                       | Application servers and the primary database are hosted by Timeweb Cloud (JSC TimeWeb) in the Russian Federation; a second server in Germany (Senko Digital) holds the database replica, cold backup and additional services; backups are stored in Yandex Cloud Object Storage — each provider's physical and environmental controls apply |
| 7   | Availability                            | Reverse-proxy health checks; Prometheus metrics and alerting; daily automated database backups with 12-day retention                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 8   | Logging and monitoring                  | Structured request logs retained for up to 10 days; no usage statistics, crash reports or behavioural analytics are collected                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| 9   | Sub-processor control                   | Sub-processors bound by data protection terms per Annex III                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 10  | Incident handling                       | Personal data breaches reported to the Controller per Clause 7 of this DPA                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 11  | Data minimisation                       | No client IP addresses are stored in the database; device logs are not retrievable through the Cloud Server; no email addresses are collected for accounts                                                                                                                                                                                  |
